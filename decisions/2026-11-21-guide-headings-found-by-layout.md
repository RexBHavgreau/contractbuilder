- **2026-11-21 — The guide's contents come from its layout, not from a separate list.** A main section is a line of capitals that follows a blank line; a numbered capital line or a title above a dashed underline is a step or subsection under the section above it. The title block (before the first blank line) is never a section. Why: the guide is plain text that people edit in a text editor, so a second list of headings would drift from it. Consequence: a new heading must follow a blank line and be written in capitals (for a main section) to appear in the contents; a capitals line inside a sentence does not.
