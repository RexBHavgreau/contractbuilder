# T-064-B Cutting a document into components (pure rules)

- **Status:** open
- **Size:** S
- **Agent:** any
- **Needs:** T-064-A
- **Needed by:** T-064-D
- **Touches:** `import_split.py` (new), `tests/test_import_split.py` (new)
- **Size gate:** yes

- [ ] A Tk-free function cuts the block list at the chosen heading level into pieces,
      each with a name (from its heading, else its first words, made unique), its
      Markdown and its paragraph styles, kept in step the way `paragraph_styles.sync_styles`
      does when the cuts move
- [ ] Moving a cut, merging two neighbours and splitting a piece at a block are plain
      functions; the pieces joined always equal the whole
- [ ] Tests for no headings, a first piece before any heading, two headings in a row,
      and duplicate heading names
