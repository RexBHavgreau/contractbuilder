Note 2026-11-21: `component_editor/clipboard_actions.py` (a mixin; the wording box's Copy, Cut and Paste, and a right-click menu). **Copy and Cut** put the text as shown on the clipboard by asking Tk for the displayed characters (`get -displaychars`), so hidden backslashes, `**`, heading and list markers are left out and fields and `{{else}}` markers are kept; **Copy as stored text** gives the Markdown and **Copy all wording as text** gives the whole component with bullets and numbers. **Paste** of ordinary text is escaped as typed text (`plain_text_to_markdown`), so `**100**` or `(net)` stays readable and is never taken for formatting; in the raw view or a plain draft the paste is left to Tk. The raw view's frame now reads "Stored text: backslashes and stars here are normal". **The reported stray backslash** came from two causes, both fixed with tests that fail without the fixes: a selection that ended between a hidden backslash and the character it escapes put the bold markers inside the escape (`snap_out_of_escapes` in `text_rules.py`), and the formatted view read escaped stars as italic markers and hid them (`styling.py` now ignores a marker with a backslash before it).
