- [x] A new USER-GUIDE.txt section **Choosing the right kind of field**, in

      a list (one per line); **Choice** (the user picks one of several
      wordings; a slash between options lets an option hold a comma); **Yes/no
      clause** (`{{#if Field}}…{{else}}…{{/if}}`, which works inside a sentence

      Value}}`); and **singular or plural** (`plural:`)
