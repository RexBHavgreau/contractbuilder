# UT-005 Windows check of direct saved-assembly opening

- **Status:** open
- **Size:** S
- **Agent:** human
- **Needs:** a Windows build from `main` containing PR #81
- **Checks:** T-048-B

This check does not block coding tasks. Use invented wording only; the
repository is public. If anything is awkward or incorrect, record the exact
steps and a screenshot, then open a T- task for the fault.

- [ ] **Search and list:** open Existing contract assembly; search by part of
      an assembly name; confirm the list shows the assembly name, latest
      revision, saved date and whether newer component wording exists
- [ ] **Latest revision:** press Enter and double-click on separate attempts;
      each opens the latest revision directly in Fill assembly fields, without
      opening the builder; confirm publisher profile controls are available
- [ ] **Earlier revisions:** choose Earlier revisions…; confirm older
      revisions are listed and selecting one opens its pinned wording directly
      in the field form
- [ ] **Edit:** from the field form choose Edit this assembly…; confirm the
      builder opens with the selected assembly and its saved components loaded
- [ ] **Review updates:** after a component has a newer version, confirm
      Review updates… is offered; open it and verify newer wording is compared
      rather than silently applied
- [ ] **Moved or changed shell:** move or change a saved shell, open that
      revision, choose a replacement file, and confirm the field form opens
      while the original saved revision remains unchanged
- [ ] **Empty library:** confirm the empty-state message explains that an
      assembly must be saved before it can be opened
- [ ] Anything awkward, in plain words
